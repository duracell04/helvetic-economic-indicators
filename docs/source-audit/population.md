# Population — audit of 8 October 2026

Source: [FSO permanent resident population, 1861–2025](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/asset/en/ts-x-01.02.04.05-b), released 20 August 2026. Retain the [original CSV](https://dam-api.bfs.admin.ch/hub/api/dam/assets/36681960/master) and select `REFERENCE_POPULATION=POP_DEC`; the appendix defines this as population on 31 December, in persons. POP_JAN is excluded. Coverage is 165 annual values, 1861–2025; 2026 remains absent. No scaling, rounding, interpolation or annual averaging is applied.

[FSO long population table notes](https://www.pxweb.bfs.admin.ch/pxweb/en/px-x-0102030000_101/-/px-x-0102030000_101.px/) identify census-based estimates through 1980, ESPOP 1981–2010 and STATPOP from 2011. The first segment is reconstructed. The [FSO census methodology](https://www.census1850.bfs.admin.ch/de/methodologie-daten.html) documents the 2010 register census and population-definition change. These changes are explicit metadata breaks, not a claim of uniform methodology. The source CSV has no observation-status column; final means the retained published vintage, with later corrections possible.

Reproduce with `--importer fso-population`. Every source count is checked, including integer precision, correct year-end selection, checksum, historical labels and absent 2026. Duplicate years, incompatible selections and invalid numbers are rejected. [FSO OPEN-BY terms](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/bfs/en/home/bfs/bundesamt-statistik/nutzungsbedingungen.html): retain FSO – Population census, ESPOP, STATPOP, © FSO 2026 attribution. The immutable original CSV checksum appears in the registry.
